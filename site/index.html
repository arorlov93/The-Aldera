Questions

Good to know.

What is a 30-Day Pass?

A box of 30 foil sachets. Each sachet holds two vegetable capsules, one full day’s serving. Take one sachet a day, or as directed on the box.

Why sachets instead of a bottle?

Each serving is sealed until the day you open it, so moisture and air don’t reach the rest of the box. And one sachet fits in any pocket, which makes it easier to keep the habit when you are away from home.

Can I take more than one pass a day?

Each formula is built as a complete daily serving on its own. If you plan to combine formulas, take medication, or are pregnant or nursing, ask your healthcare provider first.

How is every batch tested?

An independent laboratory tests each batch for ingredient identity, potency, heavy metals and microbiology. The QR code on the box opens the report for that exact lot.

Are The Aldera products FDA approved?

Dietary supplements are not approved by the FDA before they are marketed. Our products are made in FDA-registered facilities that follow current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP).

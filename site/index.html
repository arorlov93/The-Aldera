What does “lab report on every box” mean?

Every production batch is tested by an independent ISO 17025 laboratory — identity, potency, heavy metals, microbiology. Each box carries a QR code that opens the certificate for your exact lot. Not a marketing badge: the actual document.

Are the products really sugar-free?

Yes. Sweetened with stevia, erythritol and prebiotic fiber, flavored with real fruit. Zero grams of sugar, nothing artificial.

Is your saffron real?

Yes — genuine Crocus sativus, HPLC-verified. Much of the “saffron” on the market is actually gardenia extract; we test every lot specifically to rule that out, and we print the proof.

Every order comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. Email info@thealdera.com or message us on WhatsApp — no forms, no drama, full refund.

Where are products made?

2. What you CAN say: supports mood · minerals for skin & hair · supports focus · hydration & energy for your day · sugar-free · honest doses, batch-documented.

**Want more?** Videos that sell get Spark Ads budget from us — your content, promoted on our dime. 2+ selling videos = ambassador: monthly free stock + your own discount code.

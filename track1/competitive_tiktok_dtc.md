## 1.B Price reality on TikTok Shop

**Verdict: the platform's center of gravity for supplements is $15–28 *transacted* price, achieved by anchoring at $30–55 and discounting 38–50%. A $24.95–29.95 everyday price with no discount theater is above the impulse sweet spot.**

Evidence:
- The **$20–30 range is the impulse-conversion sweet spot**, sitting below the "consideration threshold" where consumers pause to research; products under $30 convert viewers with zero prior purchase intent. Source: https://www.measureprotocol.com/insights/tiktok-shop-impulse-purchase-behavior-ecommerce-search-intent
- Average TikTok Shop order ~$59, but two in five shoppers spent under $50 per purchase; ~24% report impulse purchases. Source: https://www.retaildive.com/news/us-shoppers-averaged-700-on-tiktok-shop-in-the-last-year/743104
- Actual transacted prices of category leaders:
  - Toplux Magnesium Glycinate: **$14.97** (anchor $29.97, −50%) — https://www.tiktok.com/shop/pdp/magnesium-glycinate-supplement-525mg-by-toplux-for-sleep-muscle-health/1729445237713375278
  - Toplux Magnesium Complex: **~$15.55** at 1M+ units — https://www.fastmoss.com/blog/top-selling-products-tiktok-shop-us-q1-2026/
  - Goli ACV gummies: **$15.83 (−40%) at 182.8K sold**; 3-pack $24.97 (−46%, anchor $46.52) at 126.3K sold, 4.6 stars; another multi-pack $30.50 (−38%) at 125.2K sold — https://shop.tiktok.com/us/pdp/apple-cider-vinegar-gummies-by-goli-zero-sugar-vegan-b12/1729625817788224007
  - Sea moss gummies: **$28.99** on TikTok Shop vs $49.99 website (−42%) — https://shop.tiktok.com/us/k/best-sea-moss-gummies-to-buy
  - Ashwagandha+Lion's Mane gummies: **$27.50** (anchor $55, −50%) — https://www.tiktok.com/shop/k/ashwangandha-and-lion-mane-gummies
  - Everyday Dose: $26.99; RYZE kit: $44.99 (brand-awareness exception) — https://shop.tiktok.com/us/k/mushroom-coffee
  - Micro Ingredients' top SKU (collagen) averages ~$31.70/unit — higher AOV works with big pack sizes / bulk framing — https://www.tabcut.com/blog/post/6m-from-tiktok-shop-in-last-30-days1 (as cited: $6.1M/30 days, 193K units)
- **Flash-sale/discount norms:** flash sales typically up to 60% off (clearance to 85%); sustainable promos 15–25%; launch playbook = short (2–4h) flash at 20%+ off, price back to normal within 48h to protect perceived value; tiered quantity discounts (buy 2 get 15%) to raise AOV. One supplement brand's flash-sale framework lifted weekend GMV +340% with AOV $18 higher than standard promos. Sources: https://socialtale.co/blog/tiktok-shop-promotions-guide ; https://tslagency.co.uk/tiktok-shop-flash-sale-strategy-margin/ ; https://bemomentiq.com/blog/tiktok-shop-coupon-discount-strategy-8-promotion-frameworks
- **Implication for The Aldera (ESTIMATE):** list at $39.95–49.95 anchor, transact at $19.95–27.95 on TikTok Shop. Selling flat at $29.95 with no visible discount is fighting the platform's mechanics; selling at a $24.95 anchor leaves no room for the discount theater the platform rewards.

## 1.C Creator commission norms

- Sellers set commissions themselves; allowable range 1–80%, practical range 5–50%; **15–20% is the general sweet spot across categories**. Source: https://www.shortformnation.com/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-commission-rates-what-brands-should-offer-2026-data
- Supplements/wellness benchmarks vary by source: **12–18%** (one benchmark, justified by high repeat rates), **15–25%** (beauty+supplements typical), and **15–30%+ at the high end** — with targeted premium deals of **25–40% for high-performing creators**. Sources: https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-commission-rates-fees-payouts ; https://www.hubfluence.io/tiktok-shop-commission ; https://influencerfee.com/blog/tiktok-shop-creator-commission-rates/
- Reality among winners: creators are running **north of 20% on many supplement SKUs, well above platform average**; **Toplux pays 25%** and FastMoss explicitly credits that rate as the mechanism behind its #2-product status ("a high payout funds a large creator army"). Sources: https://www.influencers-time.com/tiktok-shop-supplement-sales-run-on-impulse-not-loyalty/ ; https://www.fastmoss.com/blog/best-selling-tiktok-shop-products-us-q2-2026/
- Platform take: TikTok charges sellers a marketplace commission on top (cited at ~5%–8% depending on period/source; ~6% referral fee is the commonly cited 2025 US figure — treat as ESTIMATE, verify in Seller Center). Source: https://socialtale.co/blog/tiktok-shop-for-supplements
- **Implication (ESTIMATE):** budget 20% open-collab commission + 25–30% targeted rates for top creators + ~6% platform fee + sample costs. At a $19.95–24.95 transacted price, landed COGS must stay under ~$5–6 for the math to work.

## 1.D Category rules & compliance (TikTok Shop supplements)

- **Category approval is gated / invite-only:** dietary supplements require pre-approval; sellers must show **FDA food facility registration or cGMP certification (21 CFR Part 111)** and a **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** per product. Policy updated January 2025. Source: https://qualitysmartsolutions.com/blog/selling-dietary-supplements-on-tiktok-shop-a-compliance-requirements-guide/
- **Banned product types:** weight-loss/appetite suppressants, sexual enhancement, testosterone boosters banned outright; cannabis-derivative supplements prohibited (immediate termination). Some "herbal supplement" subtypes restricted beyond FDA rules — TikTok keeps its own restricted-ingredient list stricter than FDA enforcement priorities. Sources: https://www.blueoceanregulatory.com/blog/want-to-sell-supplements-on-tiktok-shop-watch-this-first ; https://nutraseller.com/what-supplements-are-banned-or-shadowbanned-from-tiktok-shop/ ; https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1399532709988097
- **Claims: stricter than FDA.** Disease claims banned (as with FDA), but TikTok also flags many structure/function claims the FDA allows — e.g., a legal turmeric listing pulled for "reduces inflammation." Source: https://polici.net/guides/supplement-claims-tiktok-shop
  - Relevance to The Aldera: creator content around saffron ("depression/ADHD"), lion's mane ("brain repair/memory"), sea moss ("thyroid/102 minerals cure-all") is high-takedown-risk. Magnesium "sleep & stress support" and electrolyte "hydration" claims are the safest surfaces.
- **2025–2026 policy tightening:** revised Content Policy May 22, 2026 and new Product Listing Policy June 2, 2026; enforcement is the headline — non-compliant listings removed/demoted, accounts frozen; automated scanning + manual review of live supplement listings; sellers expected to produce COAs/label proofs/substantiation within ~48-hour response windows; repeat violations → delisting, suspension, permanent ban. Sources: https://novadata.io/resources/news/tiktok-shop-listing-content-policy-june-2026 ; https://bemomentiq.com/blog/tiktok-shop-policies-every-seller-must-know-avoid-suspensions ; https://qualitysmartsolutions.com/blog/selling-dietary-supplements-on-tiktok-shop-a-compliance-requirements-guide/
- General health-content rules: https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4545471832983342

## 1.E SKU fit ranking for TikTok Shop (best → worst)

Criteria: visual demo-ability, impulse price fit, current trend momentum, competitive headroom, claims risk. This ranking is ESTIMATE/analysis built on the sourced facts above.

1. **Magnesium glycinate drink sticks — best.** Hottest supplement trend on the platform (Toplux #2 product platform-wide, $14.23M/quarter; magnesium = "sleep-and-stress ingredient of the moment" — https://www.fastmoss.com/blog/best-selling-tiktok-shop-products-us-q2-2026/). Stick format adds demo-ability (pour, fizz, "sleepy girl mocktail" content) that Toplux's capsules lack — real differentiation against the price leader. Safe claim surface (sleep/stress). Risk: must transact well under $25 to compete with $14.97 capsules.
2. **Sea moss gummies (sugar-free) — strong.** Proven TikTok-native category with transacted prices at $27–29 — the highest transacted price band of our six after mushroom coffee kits ( https://shop.tiktok.com/us/k/best-sea-moss-gummies-to-buy ). Gummies demo well; "16-in-1" competitors give a formulation-quality wedge ("ours is clean + sugar-free"). No entrenched data-visible leader. Risk: creator claim inflation (thyroid, fertility) invites takedowns.
3. **Sugar-free electrolyte sticks — good.** Stick drink mixes are proven top-GMV TikTok Shop products (Brainista $2.97M/mo; NAD+ drink mix $2.17M/mo — https://chinesellers.substack.com/p/tiktok-marketplace-monitorapril-2025 ); great visual demo (dissolve/taste-test/"water that hits different"). Risk: LMNT/Waterboy brand gravity plus very crowded low-price Amazon spillover; hydration is a commodity claim.
4. **Lion's mane gummies — middling.** Real demand and $27.50 transacted evidence ( https://www.tiktok.com/shop/k/ashwangandha-and-lion-mane-gummies ), fragmented competition = headroom. But cognition claims ("focus/memory") sit near TikTok's flag threshold, effects aren't visually demonstrable, and combo-formula competitors (ashwagandha+lion's mane) muddy positioning.
5. **Saffron capsules — speculative upside, weak fit today.** Trend momentum is real but early (Micro Ingredients/BrainMD creator pushes — https://www.tiktok.com/shop/pdp/happy-saffron-plus-by-brainmd-natural-mood-support-with-zinc-curcumin/1729594423592129385 ). Capsules are the least demo-able format on a video platform, and the category's selling angle (mood/depression/ADHD) is the highest-risk claim territory on the platform ( https://polici.net/guides/supplement-claims-tiktok-shop ).
6. **Mushroom coffee sticks — worst for a new entrant.** The category on TikTok Shop is RYZE's (53.5K units at $44.99 vs Everyday Dose's 1.9K — https://www.tiktok.com/shop/product/ryze-mushroom-coffee-flash-sale-kit ), and RYZE wins on off-platform brand awareness funded by ~4,000 simultaneous Meta ads ( https://brandsearch.co/blog/ryze-mushroom-coffee-growth-case-study ). Taste-demo content is good, but a no-name mushroom coffee at $25–30 is squeezed between RYZE's fame and commodity $15 offers. ESTIMATE: only viable with a sharp wedge (e.g., "actually tastes like coffee," sugar-free + no coconut milk powder).

## 2.C Subscription economics benchmarks (supplements DTC)

- **Subscriber retention (Recharge, 15,000+ merchants): 45% of subscribers still active at 6 months, 33% at 12 months.** Source: https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/subscription-churn (citing Recharge State of Subscription Commerce) ; https://getrecharge.com/blog/state-of-subscription-commerce/
- **Monthly churn: supplement subscriptions run ~5–8%/month; replenishment models 7–10%/month.** Source: https://eightx.co/blog/average-subscription-churn-rate-by-category
- **Billing cadence matters more than product: supplements on annual billing hold ~96% net revenue retention at 12 months vs ~70% on monthly billing** (26-pt gap from commitment length). Source: https://eightx.co/blog/average-subscription-churn-rate-by-category
- **Cadence norms:** average days between supplement orders ≈ 30; but one supplements operator cut churn by moving subscribers **monthly → quarterly** (less delivery fatigue); EU buyers prefer 6–8 weeks. Practical read: default 30 days, offer 45/60/90 prominently. Source: https://eightx.co/blog/average-time-between-orders-by-vertical ; https://eightx.co/blog/average-subscription-of-revenue-for-dtc
- **Subscriber share of revenue:** vitamins/supplements have a 22.74% subscription-app install rate across 156K Shopify brands — the highest of any category (apparel 1.96%). Specific "X% of revenue from subscribers" benchmark for supplements: not found as a single published number; mature subscription-first brands (LMNT/RYZE model) run majority-subscription (ESTIMATE, consistent with LMNT's 65%+ subscription gross margins and RYZE's subscription-flagship site). Sources: https://eightx.co/blog/average-subscription-of-revenue-for-dtc ; https://thefoodstack.substack.com/p/the-electrolytes-boom-a-billion-dollar

- **Meta median cost-per-purchase for vitamins/supplements: ~$45.62** vs ~$30–35 cross-category (Varos data pegs supplements at ~$46). Source: https://foundrycro.com/blog/dtc-supplements-marketing-benchmarks-2026/

- Structural driver: platforms reclassified health as a **sensitive category**, restricting claims/targeting; winners are "built on retention math and compliance architecture." Source: https://ecomlabs360.com/industries/supplements-and-nutrition

## 2.E Conclusion: role of Shopify DTC for The Aldera, year 1

**Role: DTC is the retention/subscription and margin channel, not the acquisition channel. TikTok Shop acquires; Shopify converts the halo into recurring revenue.** (ESTIMATE/synthesis; each input sourced above.)

1. **Traffic capture from TikTok, not cold ads.** With supplement Meta CPAs ~$46 and blended CAC ~$89 against a $25–30 AOV, do not buy cold traffic to single-SKU pages in year 1 ( https://foundrycro.com/blog/dtc-supplements-marketing-benchmarks-2026/ ; https://commercecatalyst.ai/benchmarks/supplements/ ). Instead: TikTok bio link, creator link-in-bio, package inserts in every TikTok Shop order, and post-purchase email/SMS capture pushing the "subscribe on our site and pay less than TikTok's flash price" offer. Branded search + email convert far better than cold social ( https://topgrowthmarketing.com/dtc-ecommerce-benchmarks/dtc-site-conversion-rate-2026/ ).
2. **Price architecture:** own-site list at $29.95–34.95, subscription 20% off ongoing (category norm: LMNT 13%, Waterboy/RYZE/Troop 20%) with a 30–35% first-order hook (Moon Juice/Everyday Dose pattern), keeping own-site subscription the cheapest way to buy the brand — cheaper than TikTok flash prices on a per-serving basis. Sources in 2.A/2.C.
3. **Bundles are the AOV fix:** 3-packs and cross-SKU "routine" kits (e.g., AM: mushroom coffee + electrolytes / PM: magnesium sticks) to lift first-order AOV to $50–75, following MUD\WTR ($100–360 plans) and Troop ($120 bundles) ( https://mudwtr.com/products/prepaid-annual-90-serving-bag ; https://trytroop.com/products/lions-mane-3mo-bundle ). Offer 30/45/60-day cadence with quarterly nudge — quarterly shipping cuts delivery-fatigue churn ( https://eightx.co/blog/average-time-between-orders-by-vertical ).
4. **Best subscription SKU: magnesium glycinate drink sticks.** It is a daily-ritual, fixed-depletion product (stick/day = clean 30-day replenishment cycle) in the sleep/stress need-state with the strongest demand momentum (Toplux $14.23M/quarter on TikTok — https://www.fastmoss.com/blog/best-selling-tiktok-shop-products-us-q2-2026/ ), and the premium-stick analog (Moon Juice Magnesi-Om, $42 + 30% sub hook) proves subscribers pay ~$1/serving ( https://moonjuice.com/products/magnesi-om-magnesium-supplement ). Electrolyte sticks are the #2 subscription SKU (LMNT's 65%+ subscription gross margins prove the model — https://thefoodstack.substack.com/p/the-electrolytes-boom-a-billion-dollar ) but are more commoditized. Mushroom coffee subscribes well per category norms but faces the RYZE wall on acquisition; gummies (sea moss, lion's mane) skew impulse/gift; saffron capsules are a fine attach/cross-sell but unproven as a lead subscription SKU.
5. **Year-1 sequencing (ESTIMATE):** Q1–Q2 TikTok Shop-led acquisition (magnesium + sea moss lead SKUs) with Shopify live for overflow/branded search; Q2+ turn on subscription push via inserts + email; Q3–Q4 introduce bundles/routine kits and retargeting (warm-only) ads; treat 25–35% of month-12 revenue on subscription as the success marker (consistent with Recharge's 33%-active-at-12-months subscriber curve — https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/subscription-churn ).

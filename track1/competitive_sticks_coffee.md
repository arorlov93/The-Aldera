### 1E. Review mining — complaint themes (10)

1. **Laxative effect / GI upset** — citrate-based Calm causes loose stools/diarrhea at label dose; brand itself advises cutting the dose. Sources: https://www.healthwebmagazine.com/natural-vitality-calm-reviews , https://naturalcalm.ca/pages/faqs
2. **Stevia aftertaste** — reviewers wish brands used monk fruit/erythritol instead of stevia. Source: https://usefulvitamins.com/is-natural-vitality-calm-worth-it/
3. **Poor dissolution / gritty residue** — glycinate "does not dissolve well in cold or room-temperature liquids… gritty, disgusting-tasting residue at the bottom"; users resort to milk frothers. Sources: https://thereviewrewind.com/pure-magnesium-glycinate-review , https://cymbiotika.com/blogs/brain-health/what-does-magnesium-glycinate-powder-taste-like
4. **Bitter/soapy base taste** — "nothing that it can be mixed with can mask its terrible taste"; glycine described as slightly soapy. Sources: https://www.consumerhealthdigest.com/magnesium-glycinate-supplements/magnesium-glycinate-powder-review.html , https://cymbiotika.com/blogs/brain-health/does-magnesium-glycinate-powder-taste-bad
5. **Price of premium brands** — Moon Juice $42–44/30 and Nello ~$2/serving repeatedly called overpriced; "you can get all of its key ingredients for less than half the price." Sources: https://www.forbes.com/health/supplements/moon-juice-magnesi-om-review/ , https://www.consumerlab.com/answers/does-nello-supercalm-drink-mix-work-and-is-it-worth-its-price/supercalm/
6. **Batch inconsistency** — Moon Juice reviewers report "variations in texture and taste" between batches. Source: https://letsliveitup.com/blogs/supergreens/moon-juice-magnesi-om-review
7. **Underdosing in sticks** — Trace Minerals sticks carry only 120 mg magnesium/serving vs. 300+ mg in tubs — flagged in comparative reviews as low. Sources: https://www.amazon.com/Trace-Minerals-Magnesium-Glycinate-Travel-Friendly/dp/B0FLSJ48P3 , https://shop.athleticinsight.com/blogs/reviews (comparative context)
8. **Efficacy doubts** — "negligible effects" reported by detractors of Moon Juice powders; Nello "does not appear to have been evaluated in any clinical study." Sources: https://thingtesting.com/brands/moon-juice/reviews , https://www.consumerlab.com/answers/does-nello-supercalm-drink-mix-work-and-is-it-worth-its-price/supercalm/
9. **Flavor polarization** — "hate the taste" vs. "great" on the same product (Magnesi-Om). Source: https://www.beautylish.com/a/vzqvy/moon-juice-magnesi-om-review
10. **Form confusion / trust** — mycelium-style skepticism applies here as citrate-vs-glycinate confusion: Calm's hero product is citrate (laxative-prone) though shoppers search "glycinate"; review roundups repeatedly re-educate buyers. Source: https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/best-magnesium-supplement

### 1F. Product gap conclusion

Competitors either sell cheap citrate tubs with a laxative problem (Natural Calm), $40+ lifestyle sticks with taste/batch complaints (Moon Juice, Nello), or brand-new low-dose sticks (Trace Minerals at 120 mg). No one owns "full 300 mg+ glycinate dose, genuinely good-tasting, instantly-dissolving stick at a $25 price." The stick sub-format is young enough (2024–25 ASINs, minimal reviews) that a well-executed launch can rank without a six-figure review moat.
**Why Aldera?** "The first full-dose (300 mg) magnesium glycinate stick that actually dissolves clear and tastes like a treat — Moon Juice results at half the price, none of the laxative citrate."

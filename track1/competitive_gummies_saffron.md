## 1E. Review mining — complaint themes (10)

1. **"Doseflation" / dishonest labels** — 43% (30/70) of sea moss gummies inflate sea moss content 10–20x via extraction-ratio math (e.g., "1,800 mg" label = 90 mg actual); >80% had transparency issues. Source: https://choq.com/sea-moss-gummies-review-an-analysis-of-70-different-products/
2. **Underdosing generally** — "many were extremely under-dosed"; only 34% organic. Source: https://choq.com/sea-moss-gummies-review-an-analysis-of-70-different-products/
3. **Added sugar** — "too much sugar" is a standing complaint; Double Wood carries 3 g sugar/serving. Sources: https://shop.bobbyseamoss.com/blogs/news/are-sea-moss-gummies-good-for-you, https://doublewoodsupplements.com/products/irish-sea-moss-gummies
4. **Melting/sticking in transit** — gummies soften above ~75°F; Amazon created a meltable-products policy (Sept 2022) after complaint volume; reviewers warn "don't buy in summer." Sources: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/46508a01e243b30d118d5544b675610f, https://www.amazon.com/gp/customer-reviews/R3F3UGJN7BM47Y, https://www.justanswer.com/software/s3g53-received-order-today-gummy-vitamins-werw-mwlted.html
5. **Taste/texture** — "not the best tasting," kids refuse them (MaryRuth's); "horrible" one-star reports on generic listings. Sources: https://www.amazon.com/MaryRuths-Gummies-SeaMoss-Support-Formulated/dp/B0BBPRC736 (review summary), https://choq.com/sea-moss-gummies-review-an-analysis-of-70-different-products/
6. **Unpredictable iodine / thyroid risk** — clinicians warn iodine content is variable and gummy dosing is unreliable; hyper/hypothyroid risk with overuse. Sources: https://health.clevelandclinic.org/sea-moss-benefits, https://www.bannerhealth.com/healthcareblog/teach-me/pros-and-cons-of-sea-moss-irish-moss, https://www.goodrx.com/well-being/diet-nutrition/is-sea-moss-good-for-you

Sources: https://shop.bobbyseamoss.com/blogs/news/are-sea-moss-gummies-good-for-you, https://hebronnutrition.com/products/sea-moss-3000mg-black-seed-oil-2000mg-ashwagandha-1000mg-turmeric-1000mg-bladderwrack-1000mg-burdock-1000mg-vitamin-c-d3-with-elderberry-manuka-dandelion-yellow-dock-iodine-chlorophyll-acv

10. **Price-per-gram chaos / value complaints** — price per gram of actual sea moss varies 11,400% (17¢–$19.44/g), fueling "waste of money" sentiment. Source: https://choq.com/sea-moss-gummies-review-an-analysis-of-70-different-products/

## 1F. Product gap conclusion — sea moss

Competitors compete on inflated mg claims (3,000 mg labels hiding ~90 mg extract realities), sugary gelatin-style formats that melt in summer, and kitchen-sink blends nobody can verify; the only large sugar-free player (MaryRuth's) doses just 50 mg and uses xylitol (a dog-toxicity concern for households). The Aldera's planned angle — **sugar-free (stevia/erythritol), heat-stable pectin base, honest 150 mg extract label** — attacks the three most-documented complaint themes (doseflation, sugar, melting) simultaneously, and an honest 150 mg extract label is defensible because it exceeds TrueSeaMoss's extract-basis 60 mg while refusing the 3,000 mg raw-equivalent shell game. Caveat: pectin's high processing heat is fine for minerals/polysaccharides (sea moss's actives) — a genuine advantage vs. probiotic/vitamin gummies (FACT on pectin heat behavior: https://alphacreations.co/pectin-vs-gelatin/, https://pacificpectin.com/difference-between-pectin-and-gelatin/).

**Why Aldera?** — "The first sea moss gummy that tells the truth: 150 mg real extract, zero sugar, won't melt on your porch."

## Required with every quoted product

1. **Exact composition** — extract vs raw powder vs blend; ratio if blend.
2. For mushroom items: **fruiting body or mycelium**, grain substrate yes/no, **any carrier / bulking agent added — yes or no.** We test everything at an independent US laboratory (beta-glucan by Megazyme, alpha-glucan, hericenones by HPLC, heavy metals, micro, pesticides, ETO), so please quote material that will match its paperwork. An honest disclosure never disqualifies a supplier; an undisclosed carrier ends the relationship at the lab stage.
3. Recent **COA** for the closest existing product (ISO 17025 lab preferred).
4. Production and sample lead times; payment terms.
5. Your certifications: cGMP 21 CFR Part 111 (facility name must match business licence), FDA Food Facility Registration, ISO/HACCP; organic if applicable.
6. Whether producing **several SKUs in one combined order** improves pricing or lowers per-SKU MOQ.

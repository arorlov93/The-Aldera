## PART C: QUALITY, PASS OR FAIL

These apply to every product and have no second column.

1. **Pectin gummy base, vegan. No gelatin.**
2. **Vegetarian HPMC capsules.** No bovine or porcine gelatin.
3. **No maltitol, isomaltitol, sorbitol, sucralose, aspartame, acesulfame K, added sugar or
   plain stevioside.**
4. **Fruiting body only** in mushroom products. No mycelium on grain.
5. **Certificate of analysis for every batch** from an ISO 17025 laboratory, **with measured
   numeric results**. A report stating only "complies" or "≤1.0 ppm" instead of the measured
   value is not accepted.
6. Certificates must carry the **legal name of the facility that actually manufactures the goods**.
7. Heavy metals within California Proposition 65 limits.
8. Gummies must **not stick together** in the bottle at 25 °C and must **stay soft through the
   stated shelf life**. Please send the stability data supporting the shelf life you state.
9. Natural colours from fruit and vegetable juice. **No synthetic dyes.**
10. Every active declared **individually, with its own milligram figure**. A blend stated only
    as a total is rejected.

**On allergens.** Please describe how allergens are separated on your line, and confirm that every
ingredient will appear on the label under its common or usual name.

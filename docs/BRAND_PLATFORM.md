## Ядро (зафиксированная связка)

Философия: **Less to believe. More to know.**
Мастер-теглайн: **WHAT YOU SEE IS WHAT YOU GET.**
Принцип коммуникации: **Show, don't sell.**
Принцип формулы: **Only what belongs.**
Эмоциональная территория: **Visible trust.**
Характер: **Quiet confidence.**
Природный символ: **Alder** — корень бренда, НЕ рекламный сюжет.

## Ключевые правила (обязательны везде)

1. **Alder — символический корень, не тема.** Бренд не «про дерево», не ботанический, не «natural brand с листиками». В визуале ольха — только тонкий мотив (linework, органические формы, текстура, фото, нейминг, editorial) — **никогда буквальный «tree logo»**.
2. **Этимология не выдумывается.** Формулировка всегда «**inspired by the alder tree**» / «draws inspiration from the alder» — НЕ «Aldera означает ольху».
3. Центральное напряжение: рынок говорит «Believe us» — The Aldera говорит «**Look for yourself**». Believe→Know, Claims→Clarity, Complexity→Purpose, More→What belongs, Marketing→Product, Promise→Proof.
4. Тон: confident / intelligent / human / restrained. «The brand should sound more certain as it becomes quieter».
5. Каждый продукт отвечает на 3 вопроса: **What is it? / What's inside? / Why is it there?** — единая архитектура сторителлинга всех SKU.
6. Клеймы продуктов — только на подтверждённых характеристиках готового продукта.

## Визуальный мир

НЕ: фарма, generic-Amazon, стерильная лаборатория, стереотипный organic, люкс-парфюм.
ДА: **Modern naturalism** · **Visible information** (состав как часть визуального языка) · **Quiet confidence** (whitespace, типографика, иерархия вместо декора) · **Material honesty** (реальный продукт/ингредиенты/текстуры в фото) · alder как тонкий мотив.
Packaging-стандарт: show what matters · every ingredient has a reason · make it understandable · don't decorate the truth · nothing for show · let the product speak · WYSIWYG as a packaging standard.

## Короткая история бренда (для сайта/магазинов — использовать как есть)

> **What you see is what you get.**
> The Aldera was created around a simple idea: you shouldn't have to take a wellness product on faith. We believe you deserve to understand what you're choosing — what is inside, why it belongs there, and what you're actually getting.
> Inspired by the grounded simplicity of the alder tree, The Aldera brings a more straightforward approach to modern wellness. No unnecessary complexity. No need to hide the product behind the promise. Just clear formulas, purposeful ingredients and information that helps you make your own choice.
> Because trust shouldn't come from how convincingly a brand speaks. It should come from what the brand is willing to show.

## Name story (для сайта/брифов)

> The name draws inspiration from alder — a tree rooted in the natural world. The connection isn't a claim that a tree makes a product better. It represents something simpler: grounded, natural, adaptable, real. The tree is the root. The brand is what grows from it. Nature doesn't need to pretend. Neither should the products we choose.

## One-minute version (ответ «What is The Aldera?» — интервью/питчи)

The Aldera is a modern wellness brand built around a simple idea: you shouldn't have to take a product on faith. Inspired by the grounded simplicity of the alder tree, we believe in clear formulas, purposeful ingredients and information that helps people understand what they're choosing. We don't believe complexity automatically makes a product better. We don't believe louder claims make a brand more trustworthy. And we don't believe trust should be demanded — it should be earned by showing what's there. Less to believe. More to know. What You See Is What You Get.

## Брендовая архитектура

ROOT (Alder: nature, grounding, origin) → PHILOSOPHY (Less to believe. More to know.) → PROMISE (What You See Is What You Get.) → BEHAVIOR (Show, don't sell.)

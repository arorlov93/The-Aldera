# THE ALDERA — Brand & Packaging Design Brief

**Client:** The Aldera (ISP Group LLC, Florida, USA) · thealdera.com
**Scope:** Logo + brand identity + print-ready packaging for 6 supplement SKUs
**Budget agreed:** $250 (30% start / 70% on final approved files) · **Timeline:** please propose your realistic timeline for each milestone below

---

## 1. The brand

The Aldera is a premium US dietary supplement brand launching October 2026 (TikTok Shop, Shopify, Amazon later). The brand is built on one idea:

> **"What's printed on the label is what's inside."**

Most supplement brands underdose and hide behind "proprietary blends". We print the honest dose, and every box carries a QR code that opens the independent lab report for that exact batch. The design must radiate this honesty: clean, confident, premium, nothing to hide.

**Personality:** honest lab meets modern wellness. Premium but warm. NOT a pharmacy, NOT a neon sports brand, NOT a cute pastel gummy brand.

## 2. Visual direction

- **Palette:** noble deep green as the lead color + cream/ivory ground + restrained gold accent. Working references (adjust as needed): deep green `#2E6B4F`, darker forest `#1E4A36` / `#14291C`, cream `#F7F5EF`, gold `#A8853B`. Per-SKU flavor accent colors are welcome within this system.
- **Typography:** an elegant serif for the brand/display (reference: Fraunces) + a clean humanist sans for information (reference: Karla). You may propose equivalents — must be licensed for commercial use.
- **Avoid:** pink as a lead color, neon, clutter, fake medical badges, AI-generic gradients.
- **The QR code block is a hero element**, not a footnote: caption "Scan your batch lab results" — make it a visible, branded feature on every package.
- One **master design system** across the line: same layout logic, instantly recognizable as one family on a shelf; SKUs differ by accent color + product name.

## 4. Mandatory elements on EVERY package

**Front (principal display panel):** The Aldera logo · product name · the words "Dietary Supplement" · key claim line (per SKU below, with the † symbol exactly as written) · net quantity at the bottom (dual units as given).

**Back/info panel — reproduce VERBATIM:**
- "These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- "Distributed by ISP Group LLC, [ADDRESS TBD], Florida, USA · thealdera.com"
- "Made in China" (legible, near the distributor block) — *Saffron SKU: "Made in USA" instead*
- "Store in a cool, dry place. Keep out of reach of children."
- "Consult a healthcare professional before use if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or have a medical condition."
- QR code placeholder + caption "Scan for lab results"
- Empty zone for Lot number + Best By (factory imprint)
- Barcode zone (UPC-A, we provide the code)

**Supplement Facts panel** per FDA 21 CFR 101.36: boxed panel, title "Supplement Facts", Serving Size, Servings Per Container, Amount Per Serving / % Daily Value columns, "Other Ingredients" below the rule. (NOT "Nutrition Facts".) Follow the standard US supplement layout exactly.

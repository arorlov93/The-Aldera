## PART A. REQUIREMENTS FOR BOTH PRODUCTS

### A1. Pack format (PASS OR FAIL)

1. 2 capsules per sachet. 1 sachet is 1 daily serving.
2. Sachet: printed foil, finished size about 30 x 50 mm (a slightly larger size is acceptable if your machine needs it), 3-side seal, serrated cut, tear notch on both sides.
3. Sachet film: matte PET 12 µm / aluminium foil 7 µm / PE 50 µm, or an equivalent with the same moisture barrier. Clear or non-foil film is not accepted.
4. 30 sachets in one printed carton, about 137 x 62 x 52 mm.
5. Lot number and expiry date printed on every sachet (back side) and on every carton.
6. One lot number links the capsules, the sachets, the cartons and the certificate of analysis.
7. Artwork for sachet and carton is supplied by us. Technical drawing of the sachet is available on request.

### A2. Capsules (PASS OR FAIL)

1. Vegetarian HPMC capsules, size 00. No gelatin.
2. No titanium dioxide (E171) in the capsule or anywhere in the product.
3. All ingredients, including excipients, must be permitted in EU food supplements.
4. Disintegration within 30 minutes (USP method), measured value in the certificate of analysis.
5. Fill weight variation within USP limits, stated.
6. Excipients kept to the minimum. If magnesium stearate, silicon dioxide or rice flour is used, state the amount per capsule.

### A3. Testing of every finished lot (PASS OR FAIL)

The certificate of analysis must show **measured numbers**, not "pass" and not values calculated from raw materials. It must be issued by a laboratory, in the name of the facility that makes the product, for our lot number.

| Test | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Active ingredient | Measured amount per capsule, within 90% to 120% of the label claim |
| Lead | Not more than 0.5 µg per daily serving (2 capsules), California Proposition 65; and not more than 3.0 mg/kg (EU Regulation 2023/915) |
| Cadmium | Not more than 1.0 mg/kg (EU Regulation 2023/915) |
| Mercury | Not more than 0.10 mg/kg (EU Regulation 2023/915) |
| Arsenic | Measured value with your limit stated |
| Microbiology | Within USP <2023> limits for dietary supplements; E. coli, Salmonella and S. aureus absent |
| Disintegration | Not more than 30 minutes |

In addition:
1. We send sealed samples of every lot to an independent laboratory in the United States. Please reserve 20 sealed sachets per lot for us, taken from the packed lot.
2. If our US laboratory result is outside the label claim or the limits above, the lot is replaced or refunded. This must be written in the contract.

### A4. Shelf life

| Target | Minimum acceptable |
|---|---|
| 24 months from manufacture | 24 months, with at least 365 days left on the day of shipment |

State the stability data that supports the shelf life.

### A5. Label (Supplement Facts)

1. You provide the label text and the Supplement Facts panel in line with 21 CFR 101.36, in the name of our brand.
2. "Distributed by: 518 GROUP LLC, 7901 4th St N, Suite 300, St. Petersburg, FL 33702."
3. Country of origin on the carton: "Made in [country]", as required by US Customs.
4. Footnote marker: asterisk (*) only. No dagger (†).
5. Only structure and function statements that your facility already uses and can support, with the FDA disclaimer. No disease claims.

### A6. Documents before any payment (PASS OR FAIL)

1. Product specification signed for 518 GROUP LLC.
2. Facility FDA registration number, renewed for 2026.
3. cGMP certificate for dietary supplements (21 CFR 111), for example NSF/ANSI 455 or an equivalent third-party audit, with a number we can check.
4. Business licence of the company that receives the payment. The payee must be the same legal entity. No personal accounts.
5. Raw material certificates of analysis as listed in Part B.

### A7. What to quote

1. Price per carton for 1,000, 2,500 and 5,000 cartons per product.
2. Delivery terms (EXW, FOB or delivered to Miami, FL) and estimated freight to Miami.
3. Lead time from artwork approval to finished goods.
4. Minimum order per product.
5. Price and lead time for physical samples.

## PART A — REQUIREMENTS THAT APPLY TO EVERY PRODUCT

### A1. Dosing philosophy

Doses below are minimums, not targets to be reduced. Our brand is built on printing the full dose
and proving it. We do not accept underdosing, and we do not accept proprietary blends.

**Every active must be declared individually with its own milligram figure.** A blend stated only as
a total is rejected.

### A2. Dissolution (all drink sticks)

This is a pass or fail requirement, not a preference.

- One stick in **250 ml of water at 15 to 20 °C**, stirred for 20 seconds, must give a
  **clear or evenly dispersed drink with no sediment, no floating clumps and no film on the glass**
  after 2 minutes standing.
- Powders must be **granulated**, not dry blended.
- Flavour and colour carriers must be water soluble.
- Poorly soluble mineral salts (anhydrous tribasic magnesium citrate, magnesium malate,
  calcium lactate) are not acceptable where a soluble alternative exists.
- Provide a **video of the dissolution test** with the sample submission.

### A3. Sweetener system

- **Allowed:** rebaudioside M (Reb M), monk fruit extract, allulose, erythritol.
- **Not allowed:** maltitol, isomaltitol, sorbitol, sucralose, aspartame, acesulfame K, added sugar.
- Plain stevioside is not acceptable. Reb M or a stevia and monk fruit blend only.
- Erythritol should be minimised and partly replaced by allulose to avoid the cooling mouth effect.

### A4. Acids

Citric acid alone gives a harsh sour edge. Use a citric and malic acid blend.

### A5. Gummies

- **Pectin base, vegan. No gelatin.**
- Natural colours from fruit and vegetable juice.
- No maltitol or isomaltitol in the base.

### A6. Testing and documents

- **Certificate of analysis for every batch**, from an ISO 17025 laboratory, with **numeric results**.
  A COA that repeats the specification limit ("complies", "≤1.0 ppm") instead of the measured value
  is not accepted.
- Heavy metals within California Proposition 65 limits.
- Microbiology, allergen statement and shelf life study per SKU.
- All certificates must carry the **legal name of the facility that actually manufactures the goods**.
- Export documents must state the true product description, HS code **2106.90** and the true
  manufacturer. Our company is the Importer of Record and signs the US customs entry.

### A7. Packaging

- **Drink sticks:** plain aluminium foil sachets, no printing. Retail carton 18 × 13.5 × 4 cm,
  printed, supplied by the factory to our artwork.
- **Gummies and capsules:** white HDPE bottle with induction seal, wrap label.
- Empty imprint zones for LOT and Best By on every pack.

**Question to the manufacturer.** 300 mg elemental magnesium from bisglycinate means roughly 2.1 g
of the raw material in the stick, and bisglycinate is poorly soluble in water. Tell us which of these
you can deliver against the dissolution requirement in A2:

1. Bisglycinate at 300 mg elemental, fully dissolving. State how.
2. Magnesium lactate or magnesium chloride at 300 mg elemental, fully dissolving.
3. An effervescent system, magnesium carbonate with citric acid and bicarbonate, where the citrate
   forms in the glass, giving a clear fizzy drink.

Do not quote a formula that leaves sediment in the glass.

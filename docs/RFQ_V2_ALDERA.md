## 2. ⚠️ Carrier declaration — read before quoting

We understand the market practice of adding beta-glucan carrier material to raise the assay result, and we know that the **Megazyme K-YBGL method cannot distinguish mushroom beta-glucan from carrier beta-glucan**. Because of this, a beta-glucan number alone is not sufficient evidence for us.

Please state clearly, in writing:

1. **Is any beta-glucan carrier, starch, maltodextrin or other bulking agent added to the extract? Yes or no.** An honest "yes" will not disqualify you — an undisclosed one will end the relationship at the lab stage.
2. **Hericenones / hericenes content, tested by HPLC** (for Lion's Mane). These compounds are specific to the mushroom and cannot be replicated by a carrier — this is our primary authenticity check. If you can supply material standardised for hericenones, quote it as a separate option with the percentage and price difference.
3. **Alpha-glucan %** on a recent batch of each product.
4. Extraction method and extract ratio for each product.

Hello [NAME],

Before we release the production order we need the following documents on file. Most of

**For the creatine gummy, RVG-412, product ID 122400:**

1. The Certificate of Analysis for the incoming creatine monohydrate raw material. The USP
monograph for creatine monohydrate includes a limit test for creatinine, which is the
breakdown product that creatine turns into in water, at not more than 0.1 percent. We need
that figure.

2. The chromatogram from the finished product HPLC-UV assay, not only the summary result.
If the method separates creatinine as its own peak, please report that peak as well.

3. Any stability data you hold on RVG-412, meaning measurements of how the creatine content
changes over time.

4. Shelf life, the production date of the batch, and whether the shelf life is based on a
real time stability study or on an accelerated one.

5. The batch Certificate of Analysis with measured numbers. A report that states "complies"
without a number is not enough for our file.

6. The name and address of the facility that manufactures the product, and the Certificate
of Analysis on that facility's letterhead.

7. For Magnesium Bisglycinate 123260, a measured magnesium figure by ICP on the finished
product. The specification sheet marks magnesium as exempt from finished batch testing under
21 CFR 111.75(d)(1), and you already run ICP-MS on the same product for heavy metals. If you
cannot add magnesium to that run, please tell us in writing.

8. Measured lead values for a recent batch, and your position on California Proposition 65.
The specification allows up to 2.75 micrograms per serving, and the Proposition 65 maximum
allowable dose level for lead, which is the daily amount above which a warning label is

recall H-0037-2026 of 13 June 2025, undeclared peanut in gummies. Our creatine gummy runs on

states that the product has not yet been locked and cannot be associated to an order.

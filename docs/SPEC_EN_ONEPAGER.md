## 1. Seven rules that are not negotiable (pass or fail)

1. Mushroom raw material must be **fruiting body**. Mycelium grown on grain is not accepted.
2. Gummy base must be **pectin**. Gelatin is not accepted.
3. No **maltitol, isomaltitol, sorbitol, sucralose, aspartame, acesulfame K, added sugar or plain stevioside**.
4. Sea moss products: **iodine must never exceed 225 mcg per serving**.
5. Every batch comes with an **ISO 17025 laboratory report carrying measured numbers**. A report that
   states only "complies" or "≤1.0 ppm" is not accepted.
6. The report must carry the **legal name of the factory that actually makes the product**.
7. Capsules must be **vegetarian HPMC**. Bovine or porcine gelatin is not accepted.

## 3. Gummy and capsule quality (pass or fail)

**Gummies:**
- Pectin base, vegan, no gelatin
- **Must not stick to each other** in the bottle at 25 °C, and must not rely on a separating agent that
  shows as a white dust
- Must **stay soft through the stated shelf life**, not harden and not crystallise. Send the stability
  data that supports that shelf life
- Natural colours from fruit and vegetable juice, no synthetic dyes
- The active must leave no unpleasant aftertaste. If the raw material is bitter, tell us how it is masked

**Capsules:**
- Vegetarian HPMC capsule
- **Disintegration within 30 minutes** by USP method, with the measured time written in the report
- Fill weight variation within USP limits, figure stated
- No unnecessary flow agents. If magnesium stearate is used, say so and give the amount

**Sweetener system (gummies):** target is **allulose plus Reb M**; minimum acceptable is Reb M, or stevia
blended with monk fruit. Erythritol minimised, and declared if used.

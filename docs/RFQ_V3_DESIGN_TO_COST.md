**Subject:** ALDERA — Design-to-Cost RFQ · Target $1.50/unit FOB · Mushroom Supplement Line

Hello [Name],

We are ALDERA, a US dietary supplement brand under 518 Group (Florida). We sell on Amazon US, our own store and TikTok Shop.

This request is structured differently from a normal RFQ, so please read the approach before quoting.

## Our constraint

**Our target purchase price is $1.50 per unit, FOB China.** This is a firm commercial target, not an opening position.

We understand this price does not buy a 120-count bottle of concentrated fruiting-body extract — we have done the arithmetic on capsule, bottle and material costs. So we are not asking you to meet an impossible specification at that price.

**We are asking the opposite question: at $1.50 per unit, what can you honestly deliver?**

## What we want you to propose

Tell us the format and specification that genuinely works at this price. For example:

- fewer capsules per bottle (60 instead of 120)
- lower dosage per capsule
- powder in stick packs or a pouch instead of capsules
- gummies
- a mushroom blend rather than a single species
- a different extract ratio or a powder-and-extract combination

Quote **whatever configuration is real** at $1.50. If you can also offer something meaningfully better at $2.00 or $2.50, quote that as a second and third option — we would rather see the honest price ladder than a single number.

## What we require in every quote

For each option you propose, state plainly:

1. **Exact composition.** Is it extract, mushroom powder, or a blend? If a blend, the ratio.
2. **Fruiting body or mycelium?** If mycelium, is it grown on grain, and is the grain substrate included in the finished material?
3. **Any added carrier, starch, maltodextrin or bulking agent — yes or no.** We know beta-glucan carriers are used to raise assay results, and we know the Megazyme K-YBGL method cannot distinguish mushroom beta-glucan from carrier beta-glucan. **An honest "yes" costs you nothing with us. An undisclosed one ends the relationship at the laboratory stage.**
4. **Beta-glucan % and alpha-glucan %** on a recent batch, with the test method.
5. **Hericenones by HPLC**, if you can supply material standardised for them — these are specific to Lion's Mane and cannot be replicated by a carrier.
6. Capsule count, fill weight per capsule, bottle or pouch type.
7. MOQ, and price at **700 / 1,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 units**.
8. Lead time for samples and for production.
9. Payment terms.

## Products in scope

We are building a line and will eventually order all three. Please quote each one you can make:

- **Lion's Mane** (Hericium erinaceus)
- **Reishi** (Ganoderma lucidum)
- **Cordyceps militaris**

Also tell us whether producing **two or three SKUs in one run** improves the per-unit price or lowers the MOQ per SKU.

## Commercial terms

**FOB only.** We clear customs ourselves with our own broker and do not use undervalued customs declarations, so please do not quote DDP built on that basis.

## How we decide

We buy samples and test them at an **independent US laboratory**: beta-glucan (Megazyme K-YBGL), alpha-glucan, hericenones by HPLC, heavy metals, microbials, pesticides, ethylene oxide. Production lots must match the tested sample, and we will use third-party pre-shipment inspection.

We are telling you this in advance for one reason: **a low price we can verify is far more valuable to us than a low price we cannot.** A supplier who quotes honestly at $1.50 and tells us exactly what is inside will beat a supplier who promises a premium specification at the same price.

## Documents required before any order

FDA Food Facility Registration and US Agent · cGMP 21 CFR Part 111 certificate naming the actual manufacturing facility · business licence · batch COA from an ISO 17025 accredited laboratory · product specification sheet for counter-signature · allergen statement · shelf-life data.

Please reply in a single email covering all points. Target launch: November–December 2026.

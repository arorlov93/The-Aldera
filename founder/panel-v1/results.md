## What would flip a no

- My doctor saying my magnesium is low and recommending it.
- My doctor telling me my magnesium is low, or a clear reason I need it away from home.
- A flash sale or coupon that gets the first box to around $15 to $18, or a small trial pack so I don't commit to 30 sachets.
- A price near 50 cents a day, or a big bundle or coupon deal that beats my bottle on price per day.
- A bigger discount or a multi-box bundle that gets it well under 70 cents a day, plus a recyclable or refill option for the sachets.
- A price at or below what I pay now, around $22 for the month, or a free sample box that shows me it works better than what I already take.
- If it were a gummy or a powder instead of capsules, or if a friend I trust swore it helped her sleep.
- A real lab report I can open before buying, plus a few dozen honest verified reviews, at around $24 or less.
- A few dozen honest verified reviews, plus a QR report I can open now that names the lab and shows magnesium content by batch, at $24 or less per box.
- A creator video where she shows a real situation I recognize, like skipping my night dose when I'm away or after a late gym session, plus a price closer to what my bottle costs.
- A gummy or drink-mix version of it at around $20 to $24 a box.
- A creator I already follow showing it over a few weeks, plus a cheap trial size so I don't risk a full box.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.

## What would flip a no

- A flash sale or coupon that brings it to about $15 a box, or a small trial box I can try for a few dollars before committing to 30 days.
- My doctor telling me to take magnesium, and a price closer to $15 for the month.
- A creator video showing it fixing my sleep on the road, plus a price near $15 for the 30 days.
- A creator I follow showing it fixing gym cramps or sleep in a real video, and a trial size or single box under $15.
- My doctor recommending magnesium after a blood test or a sleep or cramp problem, plus a clear explanation of why bisglycinate alone is enough.
- A clear reason why bisglycinate alone beats a multi-form blend, plus a price closer to what I pay now, like $20 or less for 30 days.
- A creator I follow showing sleep or cramps getting better after a week with this, plus a smaller trial pack (7 sachets) for around $9.
- A real person I follow showing it works for sleep in a week or two, plus a price low enough that wasting a box doesn't sting, like a $9.99 trial box.
- My doctor confirming I'm low and naming this exact kind of magnesium, with the price down near $12 to $15 a month.
- A real flash sale or coupon that brings a month down to around $15 to $18, or a free first box with a subscription I can cancel.
- A flash sale or coupon that brings the first box to about $19 or less, or a cheaper trial size.
- A price close to what I pay now, around $20 to $22 for the 30 days, or a free sample so I can see if I actually take it.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.

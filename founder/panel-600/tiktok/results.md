## What would flip a no

- A trial or flash price around $15 to $17 for the Complex, or a small travel pack I could keep at my partner's place for a few nights a week.
- A powder or gummy version that I can drink or chew, at around $15 to $20 a month.
- A drink mix or gummy version, or a price around $15 to $18 for the 30 days.
- A powder or gummy version, or a clear statement that the capsules are small and easy to swallow.
- A flash-sale price around $15 to $17 for the box, or a creator I trust showing it fixed my sleep so I felt I needed this specific one.
- A creator video showing the sachets fixing a real problem I have, like forgetting to take it or travelling, and a cheaper price or a small trial pack so I don't commit to 30 days.
- A lab report I could open and check that shows the batch is clean and the magnesium content matches the label, plus a lower trial price like a 7-day sample for around $10.
- A real price near what I pay now for plain bisglycinate, plus a small trial pack of 3 to 5 sachets for travel only, so I could try it for my trips without a 30-day commitment.
- A launch coupon or flash sale that brings it to around $19 for the 30-day box, or a smaller trial box so I can try it without committing.
- A flash-sale or first-box price around $16 to $18, or a money-back guarantee if I don't notice better sleep in 30 days.
- A clear size of the capsule (small, easy to swallow), or a powder or drink-mix version at a similar price.
- A smaller trial box (like 7 sachets) for around $10, or a creator I trust showing the QR report actually opening and showing real numbers.

- A price near $19.99 for the 30 box, or a few dozen real verified reviews, or a small trial pack so I can try it on a trip without committing to a full box.
- Several hundred reviews with the 1-star ones showing no stomach or dosing complaints, or a cheap trial pack (5 to 7 sachets) to test on a trip.
- Several hundred verified reviews with a decent share of 4-5 stars and few stomach complaints, plus a bottle or larger-count option on Subscribe & Save at a lower price per serving.
- Several hundred verified reviews with a good rating and few stomach or dosing complaints, plus a lower price or a small trial pack so I could try it for a few dollars.

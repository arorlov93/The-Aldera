## What would flip a no

- My doctor confirming my levels are low and recommending this exact form, or a price close to what a plain bottle of magnesium bisglycinate costs.
- A price under about $20 for the 30 days, or a cheap 7-day trial pack I could throw in my bag to see if I actually use it.
- A price close to what I pay now for a bottle, around $15 a month, with the same 200 mg dose and a named lab report.
- A creator I already follow, who trains like I do, showing real results from it over a few weeks, or a free first box so I can try it without paying.
- My doctor telling me to take magnesium, or a friend I trust saying it really helped her sleep.
- A refill or recyclable packaging option, or a clear reason it works better than what I already take.
- A gummy or powder drink version with the same tested magnesium bisglycinate dose.
- Under about $0.50 a day, or a flash sale or coupon that brings a box down near $15 so I can try it once.
- A price near $15 for the 30 days, or a small trial box so I can see if I actually keep taking it before I pay for a full month.
- My doctor's blood test confirming low magnesium, plus a bottle or refill option instead of single-use sachets.
- My doctor confirming I am low and telling me bisglycinate alone is fine, or a plain explanation of why one form is enough, plus a lower price.
- A trusted friend or a creator I follow who actually uses it on trips and shows the lab report is real, plus a cheap single-box trial that I can try without dropping my current supplement.

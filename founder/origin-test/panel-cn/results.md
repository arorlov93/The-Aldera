## What would flip a no

- A blood test showing my magnesium is low and my doctor naming this kind of magnesium, or a much lower price per day than my bottle.
- A price near $15 for the box, or a small trial pack so I can try a few sachets before paying for 30.
- A price close to what I pay now, around $14 to $15 for 30 days, with the lab report really matching my batch.
- A gym buddy or a creator I trust showing real results after a few weeks, or a free sample sachet to try before paying.
- My doctor recommending magnesium for me.
- If it came in a refillable or recyclable pouch or bottle and a creator I follow showed it working for her over a few weeks, I might try one box at the launch price.
- A gummy or a powder/drink version with the same tested magnesium bisglycinate.
- A price around $15 for 30 days, or a real flash sale or coupon that brings the box under 60 cents a day.
- A price near $15 to $18 for 30 days, or a cheap trial box so I can read the real lab report and see if I even remember to take it.
- If my doctor's blood test confirmed low magnesium and she told me to take this type, and the sachets were recyclable or there was a refill pouch option.
- A flash sale or coupon that gets a box down to about $15 to $18, or a 3-box deal under $0.60 a day.
- Seeing the named lab's report for a real batch checked by someone I trust, or a friend I know telling me it actually helped them sleep, plus a cheap trial box for travel weeks.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.

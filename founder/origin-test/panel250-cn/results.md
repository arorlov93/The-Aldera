## What would flip a no

- A price close to what I pay now, around $19 to $22 a box, or a free trial box so I can see if it's any different from what I already take.
- If my doctor's blood test showed I was low and said my current kind isn't working, or if the price were close to what I pay for my bottle.
- A creator video showing the sachet fixing the exact problem of forgetting vitamins, with the price at $19.99 or less for the first box.
- A price under about $15 for 30 days, or a flash sale or coupon that brings the first box close to that.
- A price close to what I pay now, around $19 to $20 for the 30 days, or a free first box so I can try it without risk.
- A TikTok Shop flash deal or coupon that brings one box to about $15 to $18, or the two-box price dropping to around $0.60 a day.
- If my current brand got more expensive or went out of stock, or if I started traveling a lot and needed something to carry.
- A gummy or drink-mix version with the same tested dose and the same lab report on the page.
- A price near $15 for the month, or a small 7-day trial pack so I can see if I even take it before committing to 30 days.
- My doctor telling me I'm low on magnesium and recommending it, ideally with a version that isn't in single-use sachets.
- A gummy or powder drink version with the same tested bisglycinate dose.
- My doctor recommending magnesium to me, and a price closer to $20 a month.

## What would flip a no

- A price close to what I pay now, around $20 a box, without having to rely on a launch price that ends.
- A bloodwork result showing my magnesium is low and my current brand isn't working, or the first box at around $15.
- A creator video showing it fixing my evening routine, with the price at $19 to $22 a box.
- A price at or under what I pay now, like $19 to $22 for 30 days, or a free trial box so I can see if it beats what I already take.
- A price near $19 a box, or a coupon or flash sale that brings two boxes under $40.
- If I traveled a lot and my usual brand had no travel option, or if it were clearly cheaper than what I pay now for the same dose.
- A gummy or powder-in-water version with the same lab report on the page.
- A bottle or refill option at around $0.60 a day or less, or sachets that are recyclable.
- My doctor recommending magnesium, or a blood test showing I am low.
- A gummy or powder-drink version with the same named-lab report for each batch.
- My doctor telling me I'm low on magnesium, and the price coming down to around $20 a month.
- A smaller trial pack for a trip, around $10 to $12, so I could try the sachets without committing to 30 days.
